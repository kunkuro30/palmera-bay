# PALMERA BAY — Hot Pursuit

[![make.fun gta6-01](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/kunkuro30/palmera-bay/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/kunkuro30/palmera-bay/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An original 3D open-world crime game built for the
[make.fun gta6-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) bounty ("Make GTA 6").
No borrowed names, art or assets — the city, the cars, the people, the story
and the music are all generated by the code in this repository.

Palmera Bay is a sunset coastal city: a 6×6 downtown grid with a landmark
tower, a pastel beachfront strip, a coast road, sand and sea. You are a
courier who works for Marina Sol — and whose side jobs keep the whole bay's
police force busy.

## What is in the game

- **A complete open world, on foot and by car** — walk the sidewalks, cross
  the beach, or steal any parked or moving car and drive the whole map.
- **Traffic and pedestrians** — cars follow lanes, slow for the car ahead and
  turn at intersections; people stroll the sidewalks and the sand, flee from
  trouble, and get knocked down by it.
- **A five-star wanted system with signatures** — crimes witnessed by police
  or bystanders raise your heat. While a cruiser can see you the stars stay
  solid and the minimap frame strobes red and blue; break line of sight and
  the stars blink while the heat cools (about 30 s at one star, up to 90 s at
  five), police blips grow vision cones, and your map arrow turns gray. At
  three stars and up, expect **roadblocks** across your avenue. Get pinned at
  walking pace and you are **BUSTED**.
- **Three story jobs for Marina Sol** — a first delivery downtown, a stolen
  sports car to move to a lockup, and a harbour heist that starts at four
  stars — plus repeatable **taxi dispatch fares** and **8 golden cases**
  hidden around the city.
- **Combat** — fists and a pistol, with witnesses and consequences; health
  regenerates only to 50%, and finishing a job (or a hospital) makes it right.
- **HUD and GPS minimap** — wanted stars, money, health, a clock, a
  speedometer in cars, and a scrolling minimap that draws the road-grid route
  to your objective with a live distance readout.
- **Driving feel** — the chase camera eases behind the car with a touch of
  lag, the field of view stretches with speed, and the frame trembles at the
  top end.
- **Sound, synthesized live** — a sunset chord pad, an engine that follows
  the revs, two-tone sirens and one-shot effects, all WebAudio oscillators.
- **A save that persists** — money, story progress, collectibles and fares
  in `localStorage`.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move / drive |
| Shift | Run |
| Mouse (pointer lock) | Look / aim |
| F | Enter / exit a car |
| Space | Handbrake |
| Left mouse | Punch / shoot |
| 1 / 2 | Fists / pistol |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |

## Run it

The game is plain static files — serve the `game/` folder with any static
server and open it in a desktop browser:

```
node serve.ts        # then open the local address it prints
```

`main.ts` is the entry's hand-in program: on Node 22 (standard library only)
it copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to the output directory and writes the
`dossier.json` summary with the vendored make.fun library.

## Source layout

Every file has one clear job:

- `game/index.html` — the page, the HUD DOM and the screens.
- `game/src/config.js` — every tuning number in one place.
- `game/src/world.js` — the city: roads, buildings, beach, sea, palms, lights.
- `game/src/vehicles.js` — car models, the shared arcade driving model, traffic.
- `game/src/pedestrians.js` — the people of the bay and their nerves.
- `game/src/player.js` — the courier on foot.
- `game/src/police.js` — heat, stars, pursuit, roadblocks and the bust.
- `game/src/combat.js` — fists and the pistol.
- `game/src/missions.js` — Marina's jobs, the golden cases, dispatch fares.
- `game/src/hud.js` — the HUD, the minimap and the GPS route.
- `game/src/audio.js` — the synthesized soundtrack and effects.
- `game/src/input.js`, `game/src/save.js`, `game/src/random.js` — input, the
  localStorage save, and the seeded random generator.
- `game/src/shots.js` — the deterministic screenshot scenes (`?shot=<name>`).
- `game/src/main.js` — wires it all together: states, camera, the loop.
- `game/vendor/three.min.js` — three.js r160, vendored (excluded from the
  authored-source count, as the rules provide).

The screenshots in `screenshots/` are the scenes named in `shots.json`,
captured from the deterministic `?shot=` mode: the cover is a three-star
chase up a downtown avenue.
